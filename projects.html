Remember Me

A diary app for people living with Alzheimer’s. Each entry is a short letter that starts “Dear…”, with a photo and a song attached, so the people and moments that matter are easy to revisit.

My contribution: I led a 3-person team researching Alzheimer’s care and designed the prototypes, keeping navigation simple and daily inputs easy for users with memory impairments.
